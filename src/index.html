Deck to Print

Paste a deck list and get every card as a print-ready image, numbered in order, ready to upload to a card printer. Paste two lists and they share one set of cards: one deck on the fronts, the other on the backs.

1Paste your deck lists

In Moxfield or Archidekt, open your deck, choose Export, copy the text and paste it here. One card per line, like 1 Sol Ring. Only Magic: The Gathering cards for now.

Where the pictures come from
Options

2Check your cards

Your cards show up here after you press Find my cards. Each numbered pair is one printed card, front and back.

    Select a card to change its artwork.

      3Download and order

      Once your cards look right, the download button appears here.

      Open mpcfill.com

      Ordering through MPC Autofill

        MPC Autofill is a free community project and is separate from this page. Its server only answers its own site, so this page cannot fetch its pictures for you. That is why the list is pasted there. For a single deck you can also give that site your deck link directly.

        Save the file again

        Ordering from MakePlayingCards

          These steps are also saved inside the download. The printing site changes its pages from time to time, so button names may differ a little. Any printer that takes one image per card will work: each image is 822 by 1122 pixels with the trim margin they ask for.

          For personal playtest cards only. Do not sell what you print and do not use it in sanctioned events. This tool never prints an official card back.

Nothing you paste here is sent anywhere except card names and set codes, which go to Scryfall to look the cards up.
